How to Make Tom Yum

1. Place a pot of water on the stove and add galangal, lemongrass, kaffir lime leaves, shallots, and chilies. Boil until the mixture is aromatic.

2. Reduce the heat, add the meat, and cook until done.

3. Season to taste and wait for the soup to boil again.

4. Turn off the heat, ladle into a serving bowl, and garnish.
